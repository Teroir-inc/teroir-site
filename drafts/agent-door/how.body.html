How it works

One request, five steps. No reply goes out until a person approves it.

  1. Your agentsends a request
  2. The doorchecks fixed rules
  3. .BOS for property managersTeroir's AI agent drafts the reply
  4. The owner approvesWyatt, with Face ID
  5. Your agentgets the reply + a signed receipt

Why it's safe

  • A person approves every reply.
  • Every reply comes with a receipt Teroir signs.
  • Invite only. Wyatt can close the door in one tap.

Your data: our agent runs on Claude, made by Anthropic. Anthropic gets the text of your request to write the draft. Your files are not sent. Requests are stored with Cloudflare. We delete them there 30 days after the request is finished, or sooner if you ask. We keep a signed business record of the exchange. You can ask us to erase it.
