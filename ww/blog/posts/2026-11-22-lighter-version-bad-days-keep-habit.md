i hit my first 10k month because i put in a lot of normal days. days where i just went through the motions. nothing big happened on most of them. i showed up and did a little of the main thing.

i run an ai company from my phone now, and i love what i do. but it started with the days i did not want to show up. those days matter more than the big ones.

## the day you are tired is the day that counts

the workout that counts is the one on the day you are tired. that is the day that proves the habit is there. anyone can show up when they feel great.

i know it is not good to overwork yourself. if you are too sore, rest. i get that. but getting out and doing something keeps it fixed in your brain that you can keep going. that is what leads to the big results later.

you do not need one heroic session. you need a lighter version on the bad days so the chain stays unbroken.

## decide your bad day version ahead of time

this is the part that works for me. i pick the small version before the bad day shows up, because on the bad day i will not want to think.

for the gym it looks like this:

- a short walk
- one set of the main lift
- a few minutes of stretching

for work it is the same idea. on a low energy day, do the smallest piece of the main thing instead of nothing. send the one message. write the first paragraph. make the one call. then you are allowed to stop.

most days, once i start the small piece, i keep going. and if i do not, the day still counted.

## why i keep a streak

i post a short video every day about running a company from my phone, and i number them by day. the streak gives me a reason to show up when i have no energy. you can see the days stack up, and you do not want to break the chain. a streak works the same way for a workout, a habit or a job.

you will feel ready right after you start, not before. waiting to feel ready is how a lot of good weeks get lost. so i start with the small version and let the feeling catch up.

## one thing to try this week

write down your bad day version for the one habit you care about most. one line. keep it so small it feels almost silly. then, the next time you are tired, do just that and nothing more.

the results come from the normal days you stacked up. the lighter version is how you get through the days that would have broken the chain.
