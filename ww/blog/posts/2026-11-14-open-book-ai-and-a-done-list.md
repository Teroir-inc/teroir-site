i run my business from my phone. my content pipeline is automated: ai agents edit my phone videos and post them on a schedule. most days i check on it from a trail or a beach. and the thing that makes it work is pretty simple. i give the ai the page to read, and i keep track of what actually got done.

## the ai idea: open book or closed book

an ai model on its own is taking a closed-book exam. it answers from what it memorized in training. and it's really good at sounding sure, even when it's wrong. it's basically autocomplete on your phone, just far bigger. it writes what sounds most likely, and sounding likely isn't the same as being true.

the fix is to make it an open-book exam. paste in the real notes, the real document, the real numbers. then ask it to answer only from what's on the page. and tell it that if the answer isn't there, say so instead of guessing. that one line stops a lot of confident nonsense.

## what i keep noticing

i keep seeing more ai tools that let you build a little team of helpers. one agent plans, the others handle the small parts. an agent is just an ai that works through a list of steps and uses tools to finish a job, instead of only answering a question.

i also keep seeing smaller ai models that can live right on your own machine. so not every job needs the biggest brain.

the pattern i take from both is the same. the tool matters less than how clearly you write the job. i give each of my agents one job, one goal and one finish line, and i hand them the page they need to read. then i check the result from my phone.

## the habit: a done list

here's where the personal side comes in. when you're building with agents, there's always more to do. a to-do list only shows what's missing, so you end the day feeling like nothing moved.

so every night i write three lines before bed:

- what i finished
- what i learned
- what's first tomorrow

after a week you can scroll back and see you've been moving the whole time. that's the proof that keeps you going. it's also the same thing i ask my agents for. a clear finish line, and a clear record of what got done.

## the thread

the ai needs the open book, and you need the done list. both are about giving yourself something real to look at instead of running on a feeling. the ai guesses less when it has the page. you doubt yourself less when you have the list.

my experience plus ai beats either one alone. the ai brings the speed and i bring the judgment about what's worth doing and what the page should say.

## try this week

pick one question you'd normally ask an ai from memory. instead, paste in the real notes first and ask it to answer only from them. then tell it to say "not on the page" if it can't find the answer. tonight, write your three lines. see how the week feels after a few days of both.
