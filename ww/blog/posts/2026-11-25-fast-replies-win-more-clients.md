## the morning starts outside

this week it was 39 out and the sun was up. it was a gorgeous morning. before i looked at my phone i was up a ladder in the shed digging out water heaters. they're just metal plates that warm up when you plug them in. we put one in every pen so the ducks and turkeys have water that isn't ice.

grandma was out with the hose. grandpa needed two heaters too. so i grabbed those, set them up, and then went and got coffee.

then i opened my phone.

it used to go the other way. wake up, grab the phone, read every message, and feel behind before i even got out of bed. now the animals eat first. by the time i open my phone my head is clear and i know what matters.

## why it matters for my business

i run my business from my phone. my content pipeline is automated: ai edits my phone videos and posts them on a schedule. so the phone could eat my whole day if i let it.

the chores taught me one thing. what keeps things alive comes first. for the ducks it's water. for a business it's the people who already reached out to you.

## the fast answer wins

i think the fastest answer wins the job more often than the best one.

when someone asks you a question, they're ready right then. they want to talk to you now. an hour later they've moved on to the next name on the list. the best answer in the world can't help if it shows up late.

so after chores the first thing i check is who's waiting on me. not the news. not the feed. the person who wants to buy.

this is where ai helps me most. my experience decides what the answer says. ai helps it go out fast, even when i'm out at the farm or on a trail. one without the other is weaker. you need both.

## what i'm thankful for

with thanksgiving tomorrow i've been thinking about this year.

in my work i'm thankful i signed my first seven-figure term sheet. that one still feels big.

in my life i'm thankful for mornings like this one. grandma with the hose. grandpa asking for heaters from the shed. an emu out back who just chills all day.

i noticed it standing on that ladder. i have a company that runs from my phone, and there i was, looking for heaters so the ducks could drink. both felt like enough. that's a good place to be.

## try this week

here's one thing you can try:

- do one thing with your hands before you open your phone
- when you do open it, answer the person waiting on you first
- keep track of how many of those fast replies turn into a yes

my bet is you'll book more than you think. people say yes to the one who shows up first.
