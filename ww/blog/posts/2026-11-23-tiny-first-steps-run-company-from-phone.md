i run my business from my phone, and it only works because every big task starts as one tiny step i can take anywhere.

## the push-up on the couch

when i first got into working out, i was not even going to the gym. i was at home, sitting on the couch, telling myself one push-up. just one.

i did that for a few days in a row. then two push-ups. then five. next thing i knew i was in the gym every day. i've been going every day for years now.

the trick is that a big task feels heavy because your brain pictures the whole thing at once. so shrink the first step until it feels almost silly.

## the same trick for work

more clients and more deals work the same way. i think more clients come from talking to more people, not from more tools. so my first step is never "build a pipeline." it's one message to one person.

instead of writing the whole report, i open the page and type the title. instead of planning the whole workout, i put on my shoes.

once you start, the next step costs way less. you're not starting anymore. you're continuing. that's momentum.

## a list for spare moments

i keep a list of tiny first steps on my phone. there are always little gaps in my day, like waiting for coffee or walking between calls. if i don't have a tiny step ready, those gaps make no progress toward my goals.

i also drop quick five word notes into ai. something like "remind me of this" or "here's a rough idea, just save it." it keeps track so i don't have to.

## and some of my best calls happen outside

this is part of why i take calls while i'm walking. i'm not stuck at a desk waiting to feel ready. i just pick up the phone and make the call. it's a tiny step with a big payoff.

## try this week

pick one thing you've been putting off. shrink the first step until it's almost silly. do only that. then write down the next tiny step and do it tomorrow.
