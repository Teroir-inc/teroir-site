.BOS · the operating system

Your rules. Any agent.

.BOS is the open framework a business builds its own operating system on. It keeps every agent inside the rules its owner signed and leaves proof the other side can check.

It sits around the software and agents a business already uses. Packs add an industry; PM.BOS, for property management, is the first.

How it works

Every act, checked before it happens.

Follow one act from the agent that asks to the receipt both sides keep. The diagram cycles through three: one that runs, one that waits for a person, one the gate turns away.

What stays true

The owner decides. The proof is shared.

Bring your own agent

Any vendor's agent, built in-house or hosted. The owner's identity, record and rating belong to the owner and stay with them through a change of agent.

Rules in the gate

The owner's rules run in code, at one gate, before every act, whatever the agent was told. People keep the final say on anything big.

Your record, your keys

Each business keeps its own record and publishes its own keys. A receipt signed by both sides is proof each side holds and either can show.

XX.BOS packs

Packs add an industry.

The core stays the same for every business. A pack adds one industry's job types, proof rules and privacy levels, and gives your agents only the tools that industry needs.
